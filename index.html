Why this site exists

Logistics is often discussed in theory, dashboards, and buzzwords. In reality, it is about decisions made under pressure, across borders, regulations, constraints, and imperfect information.

The Logistics Mindset is a beginner-oriented reference platform offering simple tools, conversions, curated industry news, and explanations of logistics fundamentals. The content is intended to support learning and general understanding rather than serve as authoritative guidance.

What you will find here

  • Explanations of logistics and supply chain concepts from personal experience
  • Practical reference material for day-to-day use
  • Simple conversion and calculation tools
  • Notes on projects, process improvement, and trade compliance

How to use this site

You can treat this as a reference, a learning resource, or a practical toolbox. Content is designed to be bookmarked and revisited, not consumed once and forgotten.
