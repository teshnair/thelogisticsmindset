My experience spans freight forwarding, commodity trading, manufacturing logistics, and project logistics, with a focus on making complex movements predictable, compliant, and workable in real-world conditions.

Professional evolution

Project Logistics

Ongoing work with high-value, high-risk industrial and infrastructure cargo in tightly regulated and time-sensitive environments.

The logistics mindset

  • Compliance is an enabler: When addressed early, it reduces friction and accelerates execution.
  • Execution beats abstraction: Processes only matter if they hold up under operational pressure.
  • Risk is cumulative: Failures rarely come from one mistake, but from assumptions left unexamined.
  • Digitization is discipline: Reducing paper increases visibility, accountability, and cost control.
