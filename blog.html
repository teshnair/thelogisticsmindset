Why I Built The Logistics Mindset

August 25, 2026

I did not build it because the internet needed another logistics website. There are already plenty of those. I wanted something slightly different.

For most of my working life, logistics has been about figuring out what needs to happen next. Theory matters. Regulations matter. Definitions matter. But operations have an irritating habit of not behaving like those examples in the training manual.

A shipment does not care about a theory. A vessel is delayed, cargo dimensions do not match the packing list, customs needs another document, an aircraft has a limitation nobody considered, a tariff changes, or someone asks a seemingly simple question that turns out to have five different answers.

That is the logistics I know.

Over the years, I have worked across freight forwarding, manufacturing, commodity trading, project logistics, customs, transportation and supply-chain operations. I have worked in different cities, with different types of cargo and with people at very different levels of experience. Consistent across it all was how scattered logistics knowledge is.

The information is out there. The problem is finding it, understanding it and then applying it.

A new person entering the industry might hear new terms and discover that the explanation they find online assumes they already understand half the subject. Even experienced people sometimes spend far too much time looking for info.

I wanted a place where someone could look up a term and understand it without needing a degree. Where a person could calculate dimensional weight without opening three spreadsheets. Where someone checking an HTS code could also begin understanding the duties and tariffs attached to it. Where world time, currencies, containers, ULDs, Incoterms, hazardous materials, shipment tracking and logistics news could sit together rather than live across twenty browser tabs.

Sometimes understanding why something works matters much more than simply memorizing what it is called.

That is where the name comes from.

The Logistics Mindset is not simply about knowing logistics terminology. It is about learning how to think through logistics situations.

The website is also very much a work in progress.

There is no development team sitting behind it. I research the material, build the tools, find the mistakes, change things that do not work and occasionally discover that fixing one innocent-looking line of code has somehow offended three completely unrelated parts of the website.

Some sections are mature. Others will change considerably. New tools will be added. Existing calculators will improve. Definitions will become clearer. And there will undoubtedly be things I get wrong and later correct.

I am comfortable with that.

Logistics itself is constantly changing. Regulations change. Trade routes change. Technology changes. Countries change their rules. Political events alter supply chains overnight. A useful logistics resource therefore cannot really be “finished” or “complete”.

And neither can the person using it.

The Blog will be a little different from the rest of the website. The reference sections will remain focused on explaining logistics. The News section will concentrate on developments affecting the industry. Here, I want a little more room.

Because logistics ultimately isn’t containers, aircraft, vessels, documents or spreadsheets. It is people trying to move something from one place to another, usually while time, money, regulations and physics are doing their best to make the exercise more interesting.

That is the world The Logistics Mindset is intended to explore.

And this is where I am starting.
