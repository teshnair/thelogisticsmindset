Carrier Acceptance & Operational Notes

  • Dry Containers: Accepted by all major ocean carriers subject to CSC validity, payload limits, and proper weight distribution.
  • High Cube Containers: Accepted on most trade lanes; inland rail or chassis height restrictions may apply in North America and parts of Europe.
  • Open Top Containers: Require carrier pre-approval. Cargo must be tarped, lashed, and compliant with out-of-gauge (OOG) policies.
  • Flat Rack Containers: Subject to OOG approval, lashing diagrams, and port handling capability. Stackability may be restricted.
  • Reefer Containers: Acceptance depends on temperature range, ventilation requirements, and carrier-specific commodity restrictions.
  • ISO Tank Containers: Must comply with IMDG, ADR, RID, or CFR regulations as applicable. Cleaning certificates and tank approvals are mandatory.
  • IBCs: Generally accepted for inland transport. Ocean acceptance varies by carrier and commodity; often restricted to non-hazardous liquids.

All values shown are typical industry averages. Payload, floor loading, and acceptance limits depend on CSC plate ratings, manufacturer specifications, carrier rules, and load distribution. It can vary from carrier to carrier. Always confirm prior to booking or loading.
